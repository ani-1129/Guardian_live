# User Guide

Welcome to the Guardian Live Enterprise platform!

## Dispatcher Guide
1. **Live Map**: Visualizes current active responders, incidents, and geofence safe/restricted zones.
2. **Dispatch Console**: Allows dispatchers to create incidents, select responders, assign them, and track their route on scene in real-time.
3. **Emergency Alert**: Triggered SOS calls flash at the top of the interface. Click to pan map directly to the sender's location.

## Responder Mobile App Guide
1. **Active tracking**: Toggle on to begin location streaming. Background services keep the broadcast running when the screen is locked.
2. **Panic SOS**: Press and hold the SOS button for 3 seconds to trigger an instant warning broadcast to dispatchers.
3. **Incident Acceptance**: View details of assigned incidents, click Accept, and follow map navigation routing guidance.
